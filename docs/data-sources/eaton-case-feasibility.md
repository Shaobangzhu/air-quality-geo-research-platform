The first mountain-footprint extent had no selected hourly rows in either 88101 or 88502 archives. The final engineering bbox is **[-118.25, 34.04, -117.95, 34.30]** to include the documented 88101 monitor. The study window is **2025-01-06T08:00Z–2025-01-09T08:00Z**, chosen from acquired observations, not the entire incident lifetime. Jan 8 is a later checkpoint during the incident.

Hazard acquisition uses `FHSZ IN (1,2,3)`, the final envelope in EPSG:4326, intersects, outSR 4326, fields OBJECTID/SRA/FHSZ/FHSZ_Description and limit 100; no transfer-limit truncation. Official viewer → lookup app → webmap `dabc3f6d15864f1b9d3d9ff4b0778f55` established both service URLs. No guessed third-party layers.

Original service GeoJSON contains nested shells/self-intersections. Shapely 2.1.2 `make_valid` repairs topology; FHSZ is then clipped to the bbox, without simplification or coordinate rounding. The perimeter repair changes planar degree² area by −3.3293793763e−7; no acreage is derived/displayed. LRA repaired IDs 2729, 5528, 5538; their defects extend outside the final subset. Polygons/holes are preserved as MultiPolygon. These are **display derivatives of official geometry**, not a newly official perimeter. Exact download and artifact checksums are retained.
